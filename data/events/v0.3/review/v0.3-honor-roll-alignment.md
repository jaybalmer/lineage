# Mt Baker Honor Roll: a column-alignment defect in the masters table

Found while reconciling the archived per-division finals pages against the Honor Roll at
https://lbs.mtbaker.us/results/honor-roll/

The Honor Roll is five tables. Four of them are clean. The masters table declares eight columns
(YEAR, Masters Men, Masters Women, Mid Masters Men, Mid Masters Women, Grand Masters Men,
Grand Masters Women, Super Masters 60+) but **18 of its 28 year rows contain only seven cells**:

2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2006, 2007, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012 and six later rows.

The Mid Masters Women cell is present and empty in those rows, so the omission falls at the end of
the row and only the FINAL column is affected. Everything to the left reads correctly.

Consequence: in those years the Super Masters 60+ winner is rendered as the Grand Masters Women
winner. That is how Larry Freeman (2007) and Bob Satushek (2009, 2010) came to be recorded as
women's champions. Both rows are corrected to Super Masters 60+ and marked `likely`; the archived
per-division pages for 2008 and 2011 independently show the same riders in Super Masters.

Two follow-ups worth doing:
1. Tell the Mt Baker organisers. It is their page and it is wrong.
2. Any future parse of that table must compare cell count to header count per row and refuse to
   position-map a short row.
