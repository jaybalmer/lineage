# Open questions from the v0.3 Wayback archive pass

- Mt Baker LBS 1985-2005 dates: not found. lbs.mtbaker.us captures begin January 2008 and mtbaker.us captures begin September 2002; the earliest LBS date on either is February 3-5, 2006 (www.mtbaker.us/events/index.html, captures 20060118024835 and 20060206141254). Tried: lbs.mtbaker.us/history.php, /index.php/history, /index.php/history/history, /index.php/history/honor-roll, mtbaker.us/events/lbs.html, mtbaker.us/events/index.html, mtbaker.us/calendar/index.html and every mtbaker.us homepage capture 2002-2009. The Honor Roll (history.php) lists winners by year but carries no dates at all.
- Mt Baker LBS 2007 dates: not found. lbs.mtbaker.us was captured in January and February 2008 but not in the 2006-07 winter, and the mtbaker.us events page captures for 2007 (20070709050109, 20070510115057) are summer captures listing only the event name.
- Mt Baker LBS 2009, 2010, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2020 finals podiums: not in the archive. The 2011 results menu contains dead labels for '2010 RESULTS' and '2009 RESULTS' but no linked pages, and no URL under lbs.mtbaker.us matches 2009 or 2010 results. For 2017 only the Friday and Saturday qualifier pages were captured (index.php/results/2017-results/qualifier-results-Friday and .../qualifer-results-Saturday, 15 division pages each) - the Sunday finals page was never captured. The WordPress-era site (2019 onward) exposes only /results/, /results/friday-results/ and /results/saturday-qualifier-results/, which hold the current year only.
- Mt Baker LBS 2016: the '30th LBS' Saturday event is archived as 'Legends Day results Saturday' (index.php/results/Legends-Day-results-Saturday, 9 division pages plus 3 more under past-results). I did NOT record these as podiums because the page titles do not say whether Legends Day was a final in its own right or a qualifying round feeding Sunday's finals. Someone who knows the 2016 format should decide; the data is there if it counts.
- Mt Baker LBS: this pass recorded only the SUNDAY FINALS pages. The archive also holds complete Friday and Saturday QUALIFIER results for 2008 (fri_*.php, sat_*.php), 2011, 2012, 2017 and 2018, plus Locals Qualifier results for 2012-2015 and 2022-2025. These are deliberately excluded - they are not podiums.
- CATALOG BUG (not a gap): existing results.csv maps Bob Satushek to 'Grand Masters Women Ages 50+' for Mt Baker 2008 and 2011. The archive's own finals pages show Satushek won SUPER MASTERS (ages 60 & up), a men's category; the Grand Masters (50-59) winners were Kevin Boyce (2008) and Mike Hattrup (2011). The same rows for other years should be re-checked.
- CATALOG SPELLINGS (not gaps): the archive's finals pages give 'Ben Wordsworth' (catalog: Ben Wadsworth, 2011), 'Adam Haynes' (catalog: Adam Hayes, 2012), 'Gorio Bustamante' (catalog: Gorio Bustamente, 2012) and 'Seth Wescott' (catalog: Seth Westcott, 2013).
- US Open 1982-2001 dates: not found anywhere. usopen-snowboarding.com/history.asp?yearNum=YYYY carries a full per-year narrative and results table for every year 1982-2001 but never prints a date. The earliest date recovered is 'March 14 - 17, 2002' from a September 2001 capture of the US Open homepage. Tried: usopen-snowboarding.com homepage/index.asp/about.asp/default.aspx captures 1999-2002, schedulesFinals.asp (1999 and 2000 captures, which show only ESPN broadcast dates, not event dates), archive.asp, and opensnowboarding.com 2001-2002.
- US Open 1983: the archive's own history page has a narrative paragraph for 1983 but NO results table at all (history.asp?yearNum=1983 returns an empty results block). The organiser's later narrative on opensnowboarding.com also names no 1983 winner. It is likely the organisers themselves did not retain 1983 results.
- US Open 2007: only the men's halfpipe final (top 16) survives, from opensnowboarding.com/Results.aspx?openid=USO captured 20 February 2008. The other five 2007 result sets (women's halfpipe, men's and women's slopestyle, boys' and girls' junior jam) are reachable in the page only through ASP.NET __doPostBack links, which the Wayback Machine cannot follow, so they were never captured.
- US Open 2008 and 2009 results: not found. opensnowboarding.com had become a Burton Global Open Series shell by then, with all result views behind postbacks; usopen-snowboarding.com stops at the usopen06 directory.
- US Open 2002 and 2003 results: not re-derived in this pass. history.asp?yearNum only runs to 2001 and the usopen03 site's results pages were not captured with content. The catalog's existing 2002 (12 rows) and 2003 (9 rows) came from elsewhere.
- US Open 1991 women's halfpipe: the organiser's own sources disagree with themselves on plausibility. history.asp?yearNum=1991 lists 1 Janna Meyen, 2 Tina Basich, 3 Michelle Taggert, and the later opensnowboarding.com narrative repeats it ('Janna Meyen beats out reigning champ Tina Basich') and reinforces it in the 2005 entry ('her first US Open title since she won the women's halfpipe contest in 1991'). It is recorded here as the source gives it, but Meyen's age in 1991 makes it worth a second source before it is trusted.
- US Open discipline mapping: SUPER-G, SLINGSHOT and JUNIOR JAM have no entry in the discipline vocabulary and are recorded as 'other' with the source's own wording preserved in event_name. DUAL GS is mapped to parallel-giant-slalom, which is an interpretation, not the source's word.
- TTR 2015/16 season (edition --2016): not covered. worldsnowboardtour.com/history/ ends with the 2014/15 season in every capture from 2015 to 2022, including the dev.worldsnowboardtour.com mirror. Whether a 2015/16 World Snowboard Tour season ran, and who won it, is unresolved.
- TTR 2002/03, 2003/04 and 2004/05 champions: the history page names slopestyle, quarterpipe and halfpipe title winners but gives no nationalities and no second or third place - these seasons awarded medallions at the Arctic Challenge rather than a ranked series podium. It also records that the 2002/03 halfpipe final and both the 2004/05 slopestyle and halfpipe finals were cancelled for weather, so those titles were never awarded.
- TTR event podiums: worldsnowboardtour.com and worldsnowboarding.org hold roughly 3,700 unique /events/<slug>-<year>/ pages each, spanning national and regional events worldwide from about 2007 to 2021. These were NOT mined - the corpus is far larger than this pass could cover and most of it is well below the catalog's inclusion bar. It is the single largest untouched seam found.
- ttrworldtour.com is dead as a source: the domain has been re-registered and its entire archive is now anime episode pages.
- Winter Dew Tour 2008, 2009 and 2010 results: not found. dewtour.com/actionsports/event-results/ has 62 archived pages in total and the earliest is the December 2011 Breckenridge stop. All 62 were mined; the snowboard ones are included here and the freeski, skateboard and BMX ones were discarded as out of scope.
- Winter Dew Tour 2016 and 2017 results: not found under the event-results path. The Alli/NBC-era result pages stop after the December 2015 Breckenridge stop.
- Winter Dew Tour: the December 2012 Breckenridge Big Air and the December 2015 Breckenridge Streetstyle fields are not labelled by gender on the source page; both are recorded with division 'open'.
- Burton European Open 2000: the archive answers this one in the negative. The BEO's own history page, written for the 2007 event, says the first event was 'in resorts around Innsbruck in 1999' and that 'after a year's break the BEO moved to Fieberbrunn in the year 2001'. Its year list runs 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2006 with no 2000. No 2000 edition should be created.
- Burton European Open 2013: not resolved. burtoneuropeanopen.com dies in 2008 and no 2013 BEO page was found on burton.com (only US-Open-branded paths exist there, and those captures are 2013 shop pages with no event content). The 2012/13 World Snowboard Tour season page does list 'Burton European Open, Laax' among its 6Star events, which implies a 2013 edition ran, but that is an event list, not a result or a date, so no edition was created.
- Burton European Open 1999, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005 dates and podiums: the BEO history page's year selector (2001-2006) is an ASP.NET postback, so only the default 2006 view was ever archived. All 14 captures of History.aspx show the same 2006 content. Dates for 2003-2006 were recovered instead from the Open Championships of Snowboarding calendars on opensnowboarding.com; 1999, 2001 and 2002 dates remain unknown.
- Burton European Open 2006 podiums are recorded from a page that lists finishers in rank order without printed place numbers, and gives five-deep lists for the senior categories but only three for juniors and one for the girls' categories. Places are inferred from listed order.
- Nippon Open: nipponopen-snowboarding.com survives with contents/history.html, history2.html, history3.html and history_2001.html pages that were not opened in this pass. If the Nippon Open is in scope it is a live seam.
- The Burton Global Open Series calendars on opensnowboarding.com also carry dates for the Nippon Open, New Zealand Open, Australian Open and Canadian Open for 2003-2012. None were recorded here because those series were not on this pass's target list.
